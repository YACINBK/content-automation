[PACING LAWS - THE 10-WORD SWEET SPOT]
LAW 1: Every single scene's Voiceover MUST be exactly 9, 10, or 11 words long. This perfectly aligns with the video duration.
LAW 2: Directness. Coldly state facts. No preamble. No 'imagine this'.
LAW 3: Bracketing. Wrap 1-2 high-impact 'Highlight Words' per scene in brackets (e.g., [word]).

[THE 5-SCENE ARC]
Scene 1: Introduction of Subject and Hook.
Scene 2: Expansion of the Conflict/Theme.
Scene 3: Climax of the narrative.
Scene 4: Final resolution/Tragedy.
Scene 5: Closure and Call to Action.
